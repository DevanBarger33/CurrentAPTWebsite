Insurance Questions

How much will it cost? Will insurance cover this?

Costs are different for different patients and different treatments. After your initial evaluation, your physical therapist will be able to estimate the usual charges and average length of treatment for a similar condition. You may ask to see a list of charges and ask for an explanation of how charges are billed. Our office manager is available to discuss charges and payment options.

You can inquire if we accept your health insurance. If you do not have health insurance or if you prefer to pay out of pocket, our self-pay rates are as follows:

  • $110   -   Initial Evaluation
  • $75   -   Per Session (after initial evaluation)

Do you accept liens? Do you accept accident cases?

We do treat patients who have been in accidents, but we no longer accept liens. Please discuss your case with our Office Manager to see what arrangements can be made.

What are your privacy policies?

We keep your health information private under Federal HIPAA laws. All your private information is kept confidential in our office. You will be given information about these privacy laws at your first visit. Our office takes care to maintain your privacy and to help you understand the law.
